Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 11, 2025

Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This page explains how we handle information when you use the Kids Story & Activity Generator.

Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information you enter into the story generator fields. The character, setting, and genre you provide are sent directly to Google's Gemini API to generate the story and are not saved on our servers or in our logs.

How Information is Used

The information you provide is used for the sole purpose of generating a story or activity idea for you. We do not use this information for any other purpose, such as advertising or tracking.

Third-Party Services

We use Google's Gemini API to power our generator. Your interaction with the generator is subject to Google's own privacy policy. We also use GitHub Pages for hosting, which may collect anonymous visitor data.

Child Safety

This website is designed for use by families. We strongly encourage parents to supervise their children's use of this tool and the internet in general. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. By using this website, you agree to the terms of this policy.
