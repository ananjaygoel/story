How to Make a Comic Strip Using the Story Generator

Published on August 11, 2025

Love drawing? Love stories? Let's combine them! Here’s how you can use our Story Generator to create your very own comic strip.

1. Generate Your Big Idea

First, create a story. Keep it simple! A story about "a cat who wants to fly" or "a robot who loses a button" is perfect. The generated story will be your script.

2. Break It Down into Scenes

Read the story and break it into 3 or 4 key moments. For example: 1) The cat sees a bird and wishes it could fly. 2) The cat tries to build wings out of leaves. 3) The cat jumps but just glides safely to the ground. 4) The cat decides climbing is just as fun.

3. Draw Your Panels

Grab a piece of paper and draw a few boxes, or "panels." Each panel will show one of the key moments you identified. Don't worry about making the drawings perfect—stick figures are great!

4. Add Words and Bubbles

Add speech bubbles for what your characters are saying. You can also add small boxes at the top or bottom of a panel for the narrator's words (like "Meanwhile..." or "The next day...").

5. Color and Share!

Bring your comic to life with color! Once you're done, you can share your amazing comic strip with friends and family. It's a fantastic way to bring your generated stories into the real world.
