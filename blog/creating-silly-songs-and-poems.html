Creating Silly Songs & Poems in Seconds

Published on August 11, 2025

The Story Generator isn't just for stories! It's also a brilliant poet and songwriter. Here's how to get it to create some lyrical magic.

1. Use a Musical Character

Start by choosing a character who loves music. Try "a singing cat," "a robot who plays the guitar," or "a famous pop star who is also a frog." This helps set a musical tone from the beginning.

2. Set the Scene

For the setting, think about where music happens. Ideas could include "singing on a stage," "writing a song in the bathtub," or "a campfire singalong."

3. Be Specific in the Genre Field

This is the most important step! Instead of "a story," tell the generator exactly what you want. Use prompts like:

  • "A short, rhyming poem"
  • "The lyrics to a funny song"
  • "A silly nursery rhyme"
  • "A happy birthday song"

The AI is great at picking up on these cues and will format its response as a poem or song. Once you have your lyrics, you can try setting them to a simple tune!
